# MIT licensing transition

## Decision — 2026-11-21

The maintainer selected the standard MIT License, following Matt Pocock’s
`mattpocock/skills` licensing approach, and explicitly authorized applying it to
the current repository and future releases without trial limits or subscription
fees. Implementation was authorized with “Proceed” after refinement and review
of the Matt Pocock, Plannotator, and Addy Osmani licensing examples.

Copyright attribution is **2026 InsideOut AI**. Distributed copies or substantial
portions must preserve the copyright and MIT permission notice. No additional
promotional credit is required. MIT permits modification, redistribution,
sublicensing, resale, and closed-source commercial derivatives; it does not
require publication of modifications.

## Transition boundary and historical records

The working tree based on commit
`9fac7051e9876bb0b662e6700fc48040a167af06` changes from the custom commercial
agreement to MIT. Version **5.17.0** is prepared for this transition. The version
bump does not itself publish a release or update a website or external catalog.

- [Current MIT License](../../LICENSE).
- [Commercial agreement version 1.0](COMMERCIAL-1.0.txt), archived verbatim from
  the root LICENSE at the transition base.
- [Earlier MIT text](MIT.txt), preserved verbatim and reused for the current grant.
- [Earlier PolyForm text](POLYFORM-INTERNAL-USE-1.0.0.txt), preserved verbatim.
- [Historical commercial migration record](MIGRATION.md), retained with a
  historical-status notice.
- [Historical Order Form template](ORDER-FORM-TEMPLATE.md), retained for existing
  commercial agreements and marked inapplicable to MIT-licensed versions.

Historical commits, tags, releases, and their recorded licenses are not rewritten.
Previously granted rights remain intact. Existing customer agreements are not
automatically cancelled, and their payment, refund, and support obligations
remain governed by their applicable terms.

## Ownership and third-party material

The earlier migration record contains maintainer ownership and partner-permission
representations for the former commercial model; those are historical
representations, not a separate signed MIT consent or ownership assignment.
This transition records the current maintainer’s authorization and does not
claim independent verification of every contributor’s rights.

Third-party licenses and required notices remain applicable, including the
[vendored Marked notices](../../scripts/vendor/marked/LICENSE.md). External patch
acceptance remains closed under the existing contribution policy; the MIT
transition does not reopen submissions or assign contributors’ ownership.
