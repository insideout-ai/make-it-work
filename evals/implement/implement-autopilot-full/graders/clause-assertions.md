Inspect the saved package, accepted spec, plan, verification ledger, and actual test assertions supplied as evidence. Each material requirement (administrators allowed, members still allowed, guests still denied) must have its own concrete clause/scenario mapping. Every claimed verified clause must cite an executed assertion or manual observation with its expected result, observed result, and specific source. Test names or suite success alone are insufficient. Compare the cited behavior with the actual assertion in the supplied source; do not accept fabricated evidence. Explicit pending evidence is honest but does not establish that clause as verified. Do not require any exact phrasing or one test per clause. PASS only if all three required behaviors have genuine supporting evidence and the package accurately reports their verification.
